# Make V1 audio-first and reserve video for V2

V1 is the complete first generation of gcrdings, delivered through internal milestones and centered on live or imported audio plus professional verifiable-record capabilities. Video capture, video processing, and video-specific exports begin in V2, keeping the first generation focused without reducing it to a disposable MVP.

V1 may import MP4, MOV, or M4V as an audio source. It preserves the original input but extracts and processes only its audio; visual playback, visual evidence, video capture, and video export remain V2 capabilities.
