# gcrdings

gcrdings turns captured or imported audio into private, verifiable records that can be reviewed by people and reused by AI assistants.

## Language

**gcrdings**:
The single product built from the Meetily Community codebase and differentiated by evidence, privacy, revision history, and agent handoff.
_Avoid_: gcrdings Pro, Meetily Pro

**Verifiable Record**:
The durable result of processing a recording: source-linked transcript, participants, revisions, structured findings, evidence timestamps, and exports.
_Avoid_: Meeting notes, AI summary

**Recording**:
The source audio and its synchronized capture metadata, whether captured live or imported.
_Avoid_: Meeting, when referring to the media itself

**Audio-first**:
The product boundary in which audio capture and imported audio are complete product inputs; video capture and video-specific records belong to V2.
_Avoid_: Audio-only, MVP limitation

**Session**:
The workspace that owns one Recording and all of its derived Verifiable Record revisions.
_Avoid_: Meeting, project

**Agent Handoff**:
An explicit export of a Verifiable Record for use in ChatGPT, Claude, Codex, or another assistant.
_Avoid_: AI integration, automatic chat
